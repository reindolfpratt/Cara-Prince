SERVICES & WHAT I DELIVER

I work with charities, trusts, and community organisations in three ways: quick problem-solving sprints, quarterly advisory retainers, or embedded interim leadership. Here is what that looks like in practice.

Strategy Sprints

Working out where to grow (and who you're missing)

If your membership has plateaued or your programmes aren't reaching Global Majority youth organisations, we won't write an abstract strategy. Over a 4-to-6 week sprint, I map who is currently in your rooms, interview grassroots leads who aren't engaging, and hand you a straightforward action plan.

  • Clear demographic audit of who's in your network (and who's absent)
  • Direct feedback from grassroots leaders on what's blocking them
  • An actionable recruitment roadmap your team can actually deliver
