WORK HISTORY & BACKGROUND

FIFTEEN YEARS OF YOUTH WORK, PROGRAMME LEADERSHIP & DATA

A straightforward look at where I've worked, the programmes I've run, and what I've delivered across UK charities, local authorities, and national networks.

May 2026 – Present

HEAL Academy Programme Lead

TRIUMPH
London · Remote

I direct the 12-month HEAL Academy, a leadership programme creating clear career pathways for aspiring Global Majority leaders alongside White leaders across the youth sector.

I lead the cohort curriculum, facilitate monthly masterclasses, and chair peer accountability spaces where participants tackle deep-seated institutional barriers. The goal isn't just theory—it's giving participants the resilience and network to lead sustainably.

Jan 2026 – Present

Solution Space 3 Global Majority Lead

TRIUMPH
London · Remote

Managing the day-to-day co-creation and delivery of the Solution Space toolkit project.

I facilitate working sessions with Global Majority youth practitioners across London, coordinate the steering group, and write the core guidance toolkit to make sure the final output is practical and immediately usable by busy teams.

Mar 2025 – Feb 2026

Head of Operations

TRIUMPH
Greater London · Hybrid

Kept the wheels turning across TRIUMPH's core mission: expanding sector outreach, securing speaking spots at national conferences, and coordinating the central steering group.

I also managed our annual review data pipeline—gathering, synthesising, and reporting on survey feedback from conference attendees and Solution Space participants so the board had clear evidence for the following year.

May 2025 – Jan 2026

Racial Equity Programme Lead

Clore Social Leadership
Greater London · Hybrid

Directed the Leadership Lighthouses partnership programme, which supports and accelerates social leaders from Black and ethnic minority backgrounds across the UK.

Handled partner relationships, matched cohort fellows with senior executive mentors, and evaluated qualitative impact metrics to demonstrate concrete career progression.

Nov 2018 – May 2025 (6 yrs 7 mos)

Network Insight & Engagement Manager

UK Youth
London / Nationwide · Hybrid

Over six and a half years, I progressed through three roles: Engagement Executive, Network Engagement Manager, and Network Insight Manager.

I served as the direct link to hundreds of youth clubs and charities across England, Scotland, Wales, and Northern Ireland. I gathered ground-level data on what youth workers were experiencing, turned those findings into clear insight briefs, and used them to inform UK Youth's policy positions, government consultation responses, and national grant applications.

2012 – 2018

Frontline Youth Work & Mentoring

Prince's Trust (Epic CIC) · Uxbridge High · RBKC
London, United Kingdom

Six years of frontline delivery working directly with vulnerable young people across London:

  • Prince’s Trust Team Leader (Epic CIC): Ran 12-week personal development cohorts, five-day wilderness residentials, and community projects for unemployed young people.
  • Uxbridge High School: Provided targeted mentoring for students with SEN and mental health challenges; coordinated after-school sports with Brentford FC Community Trust.
  • Lead Project Worker (RBKC): Mentored over 400 young people at risk of exclusion across 16 London secondary schools.
