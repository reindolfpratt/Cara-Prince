BIOGRAPHY & SECTOR PHILOSOPHY

A FIFTEEN-YEAR COMMITMENT TO EQUITY, PURPOSE & ACTIONABLE INSIGHT

From grassroots community centres in West London to directing nationwide insight frameworks, my mission has always been singular: ensuring youth sector leadership authentically mirrors the young people it exists to serve.

GUIDING ETHOS

“You cannot design effective policy from an ivory tower. You must sit with youth workers, listen to young people, and let the data guide genuine reform.”

THE FRONTLINE FOUNDATIONS

Where It All Began: The Streets and Classrooms of London

My career did not start in boardrooms or strategy retreats—it began on the frontline. Over a decade ago, working across 16 secondary schools in the Royal Borough of Kensington and Chelsea, I provided direct one-to-one mentoring, educational guidance, and behavioural support to over 400 young people at risk of exclusion.

Later, as a Prince’s Trust Team Leader with Epic CIC and a support worker at Uxbridge High School, I designed 12-week life-changing developmental programmes, led five-day wilderness residentials, and forged partnerships with community sports foundations like Brentford FC. These early years taught me an unforgettable lesson: when young people are given structured trust and compassionate boundaries, their capacity for resilience is boundless.

NATIONWIDE SCALE

Building Movements: Six Years at UK Youth

Frontline experience naturally revealed the wider systemic barriers holding the sector back: fragmented communication, lack of representative leadership, and decisions made without robust evidence. In 2018, I joined UK Youth, where I spent over six and a half years progressing through engagement, advocacy, and network insight leadership.

As Network Insight Manager, I had the privilege of listening to hundreds of youth organisations across England, Wales, Scotland, and Northern Ireland. I synthesised their real-time challenges—from pandemic disruptions to funding cliffs—into authoritative reports that directly shaped UK Youth’s national policy advocacy, funding allocations, and service design.

RACIAL EQUITY & CO-CREATION

Pioneering the HEAL Academy & Leadership Lighthouses

Addressing inequities requires more than statements of intent; it demands systemic architecture. At Clore Social Leadership, I led the Leadership Lighthouses partnership programme to nurture and champion social leaders from Black and ethnic minority backgrounds.

Today, as Programme Lead for the HEAL Academy at TRIUMPH and Solution Space 3 Global Majority Lead, I direct 12-month leadership programmes designed to dismantle practices that perpetuate inequity, while facilitating safe, courageous spaces for sector colleagues to co-create lasting structural solutions.

CORE PRINCIPLES
