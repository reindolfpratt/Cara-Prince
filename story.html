MY STORY & ETHOS

FIFTEEN YEARS BETWEEN THE FRONTLINE AND THE BOARDROOM

I started out mentoring teenagers across West London who were at risk of being kicked out of school. Fifteen years later, I direct national leadership programmes and help youth charities make sense of their data. The rooms got bigger, but the job is the same: listen to what people actually need, and build something that works.

WHAT GUIDES ME

“You can't design good policy from an ivory tower. You have to sit with youth workers, listen to young people, and let the real evidence lead the way.”

WHERE I STARTED

The classrooms and estates of West London

My career didn't start in strategy away-days. It began across 16 secondary schools in Kensington and Chelsea, working one-on-one with over 400 young people who had been written off or were at risk of exclusion.

Later, leading 12-week Prince's Trust teams with Epic CIC and working inside Uxbridge High School, I ran five-day wilderness residentials, set up community partnerships with Brentford FC, and dealt with the daily crises youth workers face. If you don't understand how exhausting frontline delivery is, you have no business writing strategy for it.

SIX YEARS AT UK YOUTH

Listening to hundreds of youth groups across the country

In 2018 I moved into national network work at UK Youth. Over six and a half years, I worked my way up from engagement executive to Network Insight Manager.

My job was simple in principle, but tricky in practice: talk to youth organisations across England, Scotland, Wales, and Northern Ireland, understand what was actually keeping them awake at night — funding cliffs, pandemic disruption, volunteer burnout — and turn that feedback into evidence the CEO and trustees could use to lobby government and win major grant funding.

EQUITY IN PRACTICE

Building the HEAL Academy and spaces for the Global Majority

Diversity talk in the non-profit sector is cheap. What's rare is giving practitioners from the Global Majority the actual tools, peer networks, and institutional leverage to lead without burning out.

At Clore Social Leadership, I directed the Leadership Lighthouses partnership. Today, as Programme Lead for the HEAL Academy at TRIUMPH, I run a 12-month leadership journey connecting emerging Global Majority leaders and White leaders who want to move past performative allyship into real structural change.

A FEW LESSONS

How I think about consulting today

A couple of principles keep me grounded:

If a board report takes more than five minutes to read, it won't get acted on. I turn messy data into clear, visual one-pagers that make the next step obvious.

Real inclusion means redistributing power and budget, not just putting different faces on an annual report cover.

And keeping fees transparent matters to me. That's why my rate is clearly stated upfront (£30/hour) without hidden markups or retainer traps. Good consulting should be accessible to grassroots groups, not just multi-million-pound trusts.
